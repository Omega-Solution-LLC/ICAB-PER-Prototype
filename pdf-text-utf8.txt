2. Professional Skills
Technical Development
•Training modules will be developed for specific 
areas in line with IES 2.
•Each module will be made available for study.
•Students will undergo an assessment in an exam 
environment at the ICAB Lab.
•Upon successful completion of the assessment, 
the status will be updated in the student profile.
•A minimum annual requirement must be met to 
obtain the course completion certificate.
•A separate list of modules will be designed for 
the new generation ACA.
Professional Skills as per IES 3
•Throughout the training period, students will 
acquire a minimum set of skills (5–10).
•Students will be required to acquire a specified 
number of skills every six months.
•Students will document their skills using guided 
questions.
•The Principal will review and provide feedback 
on the documented skills.

3. Ethics and value leadership
Ethics and value training results
•Students must complete an ethical learning section during 
their first year.
•The ethical learning module will be available within this 
section.
•Students are required to undergo an ethical assessment in 
an exam environment at the ICAB Lab.
•A minimum passing threshold must be achieved, and 
students will be allowed a maximum of five attempts per 
month.
•Upon successful completion, the status will be updated in 
the student profile.
•A course completion certificate will not be issued without 
completion of this module.
•Students may be barred from appearing in examinations if 
they fail to complete this requirement within one year.
Application of ethics and values
•Students shall document at least one ethical issue from 
practical experience every six months, demonstrating 
application of ethical principles.
•A prescribed format will be provided to record the 
scenario, including the ethical dilemma, relevant 
principles, and actions taken.
•Students shall briefly reflect on the issue and justify their 
decisions using an appropriate ethical decision-making 
approach. 
•The Principal shall review each submission to evaluate 
ethical judgment, provide feedback, and approve the 
record.
